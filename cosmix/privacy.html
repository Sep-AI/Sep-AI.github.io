Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Cosmix: Planet Merge mobile game (“Cosmix”, “the game”, package com.sepai.cosmix) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information the game stores on your device

Cosmix saves your game progress locally on your device: best scores, coins, selected game mode and settings such as sound and vibration. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Cosmix has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Advertising

Cosmix is free and offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, to use a boost or continue a game). Ads are delivered by third-party advertising partners through Unity LevelPlay mediation, including Unity Ads and, where available, other ad networks such as Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device type, operating system, language and network information;
  • IP address and approximate (country-level) location derived from it;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

This information is processed by those partners under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (Unity Ads and LevelPlay)
  • Google (AdMob)

Your choices

Watching ads in Cosmix is always optional. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads may still appear, but they will not be personalized.

Children

Cosmix is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us at the email below and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We do not operate servers that receive your data. Game progress stays on your device. Information collected by advertising partners is kept according to their own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Cosmix is developed by Sepehr Meshkinfamfard (seppymoo). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please email sepehr.meshkinfam@gmail.com.
